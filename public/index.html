CF Email Decoder

Decode Cloudflare-protected emails and list phone numbers and links from pages you are allowed to crawl.

Enter a URL and click "Crawl" to begin.

Cloudflare data-cfemail protected emails are automatically decoded via XOR decryption. A crawl fetches at most 20 pages, skips private and metadata addresses, and obeys robots.txt. Results are not stored.

Terms of use

This tool is for educational use. It fetches pages you submit, decodes Cloudflare-protected email addresses, and can list phone numbers and links that appear on those pages.

  • Only crawl sites you are allowed to access. You must follow the site's own terms, its robots.txt, and the law, including the GDPR.
  • Do not use this tool to harvest personal data from third parties without a lawful basis and, where the law requires it, their consent.
  • The crawler requests at most 20 pages, refuses responses larger than 2 MB, and will not fetch a URL that robots.txt disallows.
  • It does not crawl loopback, link-local, private-network, or cloud metadata addresses.
  • Crawl results are returned to your browser and are not stored or written to server logs.
  • You are responsible for how you use and share anything the tool returns. The tool is provided as is, without warranty.
